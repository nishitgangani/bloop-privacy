Bloop Privacy Policy

Bloop: Water Reminder & Tracker · Seventy Seven Developers

Effective date: 13 October 2026

This policy explains what information the Bloop app (“Bloop”, “the app”) handles, where it is kept and who can see it. Bloop is made by Seventy Seven Developers (“we”, “us”).

In short
  • Your water log, goal and settings are stored only on your phone. We have no servers and no user accounts, and we never receive your data.
  • The app shows ads from Google AdMob. Google may collect device identifiers and usage information to show and measure ads, as described below.
  • You can change your ad consent at any time in Settings → Privacy options (where required by law).

1. Information stored on your device

To work, Bloop saves the following information in the app's private storage on your phone:

  • Water log: the amount and time of each drink you log.
  • Goals: your daily water goal and when you changed it.
  • Profile (optional): gender and body weight, used only to suggest a daily water goal.
  • Settings: wake-up and bed times, reminder preferences, units, theme and cup size.

This information never leaves your phone through Bloop, is not sent to us, and is not shared with or sold to anyone. Other apps on your phone cannot read it.

2. Backup

Android's built-in Auto Backup may copy the app's data to your own Google account so it can be restored if you reinstall Bloop or move to a new phone. This backup is provided and encrypted by Google under Google's Privacy Policy; we cannot access it. You can turn backup off in your phone's Settings (Google → Backup).

3. Notifications

If you allow notifications, Bloop uses them only to remind you to drink water. Reminders are scheduled on your phone; no information is sent anywhere to schedule them.

4. Advertising (Google AdMob)

Bloop is free and supported by ads served by Google AdMob, a service of Google LLC. To show, personalise (where allowed) and measure ads, and to prevent fraud, Google may collect and process information such as:

InformationPurpose
Advertising ID and other device identifiersAd serving, frequency limits, measurement, personalisation (with consent where required)

Your water log and profile are not shared with Google or any advertiser. Learn more in How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services and Google's advertising policies.

5. Your consent and choices

  • European Economic Area, United Kingdom and Switzerland: on first use, Bloop asks for your consent through Google's consent form before personalised ads are shown. You can change or withdraw consent at any time in Settings → Privacy options.
  • United States: where state privacy laws apply, you can opt out of the sale or sharing of personal information for targeted advertising through the same privacy options.
  • Everyone: you can reset your advertising ID or opt out of ads personalisation in your phone's Settings (Google → Ads / Privacy → Ads).

6. Keeping and deleting your data

Your data stays on your phone until you delete it. To delete everything, uninstall Bloop or clear the app's storage in your phone's Settings. Because we do not hold any of your data, there is nothing for us to delete on our side. For data collected by Google for advertising, see My Ad Center and Google's Privacy Policy.

7. Children

Bloop is a general-audience app and is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided information through the app, contact us and we will help.

8. Health information

Bloop is a general wellness tool and does not provide medical advice. Suggested water goals are general estimates. If you have a medical condition that affects fluid intake, follow your doctor's advice.

9. Security

Your data is kept in the app's private storage, protected by Android's app sandbox. No method of storage is perfectly secure, so we recommend keeping your phone locked with a PIN, pattern or password.

10. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy, for example when the app gains new features. The effective date above will change and, for significant changes, we will let you know in the app.
